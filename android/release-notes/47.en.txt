- Chapter 11, Road and traffic signs: 66 new questions, 216 in all. The chapter named each sign; now it also asks how the system works - what a blue circle orders, what a brown sign points to, what a triangle warns of - and tests traffic lights, police and arm signals, motorway signals and studs, and the markings painted on the road, with photographs of the real thing.
- Twenty-two of them put four signs in front of you and ask which one means what, the way the real test does.
