EnglishMaster — Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

EnglishMaster is an English-learning app for Android. This policy explains what we store, why, and how you can delete it. The same text is shown inside the app (My account → Privacy policy).

What we collect

Your name, email address and password (stored securely by our sign-in service, never in plain text); your answers, progress, exam results and certificates; and a log of important account events (for example sign-up, exams and staff actions). We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising IDs. The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics.

How we use it

Only to run the service: to mark your answers, show your progress, run exams, issue certificates and keep the service secure.

Who can see it

Your teachers and the app administrators can see your name, email address and progress. Anyone who has a certificate code can check that certificate (name, level, score and date). We never sell your data. Data is stored with our hosting provider Supabase in the European Union (Ireland) and is encrypted in transit.

On your device

Downloaded questions and answers you give offline are kept on your device until they are sent. Your sign-in is stored encrypted.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: My account → Delete account. Your account and all data linked to it (answers, progress, exam results, certificates and your activity-log entries) are deleted straight away. If you can no longer open the app, email us from the address you registered with and we will delete the account for you.

Children

Learners under 13 should use the app with a parent, guardian or teacher.
