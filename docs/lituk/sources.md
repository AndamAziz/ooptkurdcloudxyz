# Life in the UK: source assessment

Checked 2026-10-22. Only sources whose terms allow the intended use may feed the question bank.

| Source | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Official handbook, *Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents*, 3rd ed. (TSO, ISBN 9780113413553) | **Needed, not yet available** | Sold by TSO (PDF, eBook, paperback). Required to mark any question `verified`. TSO's eBook listing says the EU-membership and monarchy sections are out of date, were removed from later prints, and are not examined. |
| GOV.UK (`gov.uk/life-in-the-uk-test`, `/what-happens-test`, election, courts, MOT and driving pages) | **Usable for corroboration** | Confirms the test format (24 questions, 45 minutes, 75%) and current-law facts; evidence recorded in `content/lituk/question-meta.json` as `corroborated_external`. |
| lifeintheuktestweb.co.uk (Exams 1-17) | **Not usable without written permission** | Its terms and conditions (`/terms-and-conditions`) state that any redistribution or reproduction of part or all of its content is prohibited, that visitors may not download, copy, print or store any material, and that content may not be distributed or commercially exploited without the owner's express written permission. No exam page was extracted; the index and terms pages fetched while checking were deleted. |
| Open HTML edition of the handbook reported in 2013 (Public.Resource.Org / furd.org) | **Not available** | The reported location `furd.org/library/comm44a` returns 404; edition and licence could not be confirmed. |

## Permission needed for lifeintheuktestweb.co.uk

Express written permission from the site owner (contact form: `https://lifeintheuktestweb.co.uk/contact-us/`)
covering: (1) reading and storing the questions of Exams 1-17 for research, (2) recording their exam and
question numbers for traceability, and (3) using the facts they test to write original questions for this
app (and, if wanted, (4) reusing their wording or explanations, with the attribution they require). Until then
the site is not used as a source, and no question in the bank is derived from it.
